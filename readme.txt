WizardTD v1.0.0

A tower defense game where enemies are spawned in many increasingly more difficult waves, culminating in a boss fight!
Enemies of varying sizes and speeds make their way (from multiple directions) towards your central fortress.
Towers shoot and destroy enemies, and can be placed on plots, and can also be upgraded and destroyed.
The five tower options are : Archer Tower, Fire Tower, Cannon, and Tesla Tower and Mage Tower.
Towers can be upgraded with gold (earned by killing enemies), with multiple upgrade paths available for towers!
The Game can be paused and resumed, and the speed can also be changed between 0.5x, 1x (default), and 2x.

This game is accessible, having the options of key binds to help those with hand mobility issues
